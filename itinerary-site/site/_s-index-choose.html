How to choose

  • Gora Kadan and peak Kyoto colour are the pointItinerary 1 — Peak Momiji ClassicThe seeds trip, and the cuts brought it from $36.2k to $34.1k without touching a hotel
  • You want the most wildlife for the moneyItinerary 2 — Crafts, Crab & Snow MonkeysFlying squirrels and two macaque troops on one rail line, plus crab week and Kenrokuen at peak, at $29.7k
  • It has to be the Ezo momonga specificallyItinerary 3 — Wildlife North + Momiji FinaleNow $29.3k and inside budget. But Karuizawa puts a flying squirrel on itineraries 1, 2 and 5 for $170, so ask whether it is the species or the animal you want
  • Warmth, a road trip and the lowest numberItinerary 4 — Southern WarmthCheapest at $28.2k and the only all-temp-band option — but the only route with no flying squirrel and no owl café
  • Once-in-a-decade moodItinerary 5 — The Grand TourEverything, still over budget at $35.5k, and Karuizawa has weakened its main argument
