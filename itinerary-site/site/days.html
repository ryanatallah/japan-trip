  • The wasabi: Joren Falls, a 25m drop in a basalt gorge with tatami-ishi terraces free from the deck; a working farm at Wasabi no Ōmiya where the pickling session runs January to November at ¥1,650 a head; and the Ikadaba terraces, fifteen hectares and about 1,500 paddies, the largest such landscape in Japan.
