Sat 7 Land at dawn

Saturday · based at Aman Tokyo · Tokyo

Fixed points

  1. 04:50 NH107 lands at Haneda
  2. 15:00 Room available at Aman TokyoRequest early check-in at booking; failing that, the spa and lounge take you.

Getting there

06:00 leave 25 min

  • car Haneda → Aman Tokyo25 min Private transfer. Empty roads at that hour — this is the fastest this journey ever is.

Meals

  • Breakfast The hotel 07:00Open You will be in the building nine hours before the room is. Breakfast, then the spa.
  • Lunch NearbyOpen
  • Dinner Nothing bookedOpen Correct. You have been awake for a very long time, and L'Effervescence is shut on Sundays and Mondays anyway.
  • The ten-hour gap is the real logistics problem of this trip, and it is on day one. You clear Haneda around 05:45 and the room is not ready until 15:00. Three things fix it, in order: ask Aman for early check-in when you book (a dawn arrival is exactly the case they accommodate); use the spa, which has the stone baths and the 30m pool and does not care what time it is; and take the Imperial Palace East Gardens at opening — ten minutes on foot, free, and open on Saturdays.
  • Do not schedule anything with a reservation on this day. The gentle evening is the plan, and it is the right one.

Sat 14 The Kiso valley, and the shuttle you must catch

Saturday · ends at Tobira Onsen Myojinkan · → Tobira Onsen

Fixed points

  1. 11:00 Gora Kadan check-out
  2. 15:15 Myojinkan shuttle departs Matsumoto StationEast exit, down two escalators, right to the rotary by the police box. The only other departure is 16:30.

Getting there

10:35 leave by 5h 18

  • rail Gora Kadan → Odawara1h Tozan railway to Hakone-Yumoto, then Odakyu to Odawara.
  • rail Odawara → Nagoya1h 10 Hikari — about 1h10. Nozomi does not stop at Odawara, so this is Hikari or the very slow Kodama.
  • rail Nagoya → Matsumoto1h 28 Limited Express Shinano up the Kiso valley, about 1h28, roughly hourly.
  • bus Matsumoto Station → Myojinkan40 min The shuttle. 35–45 minutes up the valley to 1,050m.

Meals

  • Breakfast Gora KadanIncluded
  • Lunch On the Shinano, or at NagoyaOpen Buy it at Nagoya during the change — there is nothing at Matsumoto worth the tight connection.
  • Dinner Myojinkan — kaiseki or the French menuIncluded Which kitchen you eat in is chosen when you book, not on the day. That decision has to be made across all three nights, in advance, with the gluten-free brief attached.
  • The one genuinely tight day, and it is tight against a shuttle rather than a train. Adding the legs up — an hour down to Odawara, 1h10 to Nagoya, 1h28 to Matsumoto, plus three connections — the latest you can leave Gora Kadan and still make the 15:15 is 10:35, twenty-five minutes before check-out. That is the latest, not the plan: go at 10:00 and you have half an hour of slack on a day with three changes.
  • Neither outcome is a disaster, but they are different days. The 15:15 gives you an hour of daylight in the valley and an unhurried first onsen. The 16:30 gets you in more or less straight to dinner. Decide which you want before you book the shuttle, because it is reservation-only either way.
  • The escape hatch is a taxi: 30 minutes from Matsumoto Station, about ¥7,000, no fixed departure. Worth holding in reserve if the Shinano runs late.
