Or swap the Alps for the coast. Beniya Mukayu in Kaga is below, and on these earlier dates it is arguably better than it was: snow crab opens 6 November, so 14–16 November is already in season, and Kenrokuen peaks mid-November — you would catch it almost exactly. It is also two hours from Kyoto rather than three. The reason it is not the default is unchanged: Itinerary 2 already spends three nights in that exact place.

The verdict

What the re-date costs. Central Kyoto — Tofuku-ji, Eikandō, Kiyomizu — will be part-turned rather than blazing, because 2026 is forecast late and those temples are the last to go. The Jingu Gaien ginkgo in Tokyo are early too. And your final Kyoto day falls on the 21–23 November holiday weekend, the busiest stretch of the season.

What it buys. Hakone at its own mid-November peak instead of past it. Kamikochi and the Venus Line still open in the Alps. Sowaka at pre-peak rates, which is most of why this is now about $1,800 cheaper than Itinerary 1. The flying squirrels comfortably inside their season rather than in its closing week. And Takao, Kurama, Kibune, Ohara and Rurikoin — all of which are at their best precisely when you are there, and all of which most visitors skip because they are chasing the central temples.

You will see real autumn colour. It will just be in the hills rather than in Higashiyama.
