Mon 23Alps

The mountain day, honestly framed: Kamikochi closed on 15 November and the Venus Line with it, so the option that still works is the Shinhotaka Ropeway — year-round, about two hours away, and it lifts you to 2,150m above the snow line. Or don't go anywhere. Three nights at a ryokan of this kind is partly the point, and this is the day the trip stops moving.
