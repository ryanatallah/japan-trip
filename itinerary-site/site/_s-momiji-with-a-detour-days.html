Day by day

Sat Nov 7 → Sun Nov 22, 2026 · 14 nights

Sat 7✈

SFO→HND, ANA The Room

Sun 8Tokyo

Land, private transfer, gentle evening. Palace Hotel Tokyo.

Mon 9Tokyo

Meiji Jingu. The Jingu Gaien ginkgo are still turning this early — they gold up around 20 Nov, so expect green-to-yellow rather than the corridor of gold. Afternoon at Akiba Fukurou, the owl café. L'Effervescence dinner.

Tue 10Tokyo · Ome

Hirata sword smithing — forge a small katana with the husband-and-wife smiths, engraved and shipped home.

Wed 11Karuizawa

Flying squirrels. Shinkansen out after lunch, Picchio's musasabi watching tour at dusk, back in Tokyo by evening. Comfortably inside the season now rather than scraping its last week.

Thu 12Hakone

Romancecar or private transfer. Gora Kadan — gluten-free kaiseki in the room, cypress onsen. Hakone peaks mid-November, so these two nights are now better timed than they were.

Fri 13Hakone

Open-Air Museum, Lake Ashi with the foliage at its best. Lunch at Itoh Dining by Nobu. Second kaiseki at the ryokan.

Sat 14→ Tobira Onsen

Odawara→Nagoya, then the Limited Express Shinano up the Kiso valley to Matsumoto — about 3h20. Tobira Onsen Myojinkan, Relais & Châteaux, at 1,000m. Kaiseki or the French menu — chosen when you book, not on the day.

Sun 15Matsumoto

Matsumoto Castle — an original 1594 keep, black and white against the snow line. Nakamachi's kura storehouse street, and the city art museum for the Yayoi Kusama collection (open — it closes Mondays, not Sundays).

Mon 16Alps

The day the earlier dates pay for themselves: the Venus Line is still open (it shuts on the 20th) for the Utsukushigahara plateau, and the Shinhotaka Ropeway runs year-round to 2,150m. Or stay put — three nights at a ryokan like this is partly the point.

Tue 17→ Kyoto

Shinano down to Nagoya, shinkansen to Kyoto — about 3h. Sowaka in Gion, at pre-peak rates rather than peak ones. Evening Gion walk.

Wed 18Kyoto · Takao

Takao, and this is the day that answers the foliage question. The gorge on the Kiyotaki river peaks early-to-mid November, so it should be at or near its best: Jingo-ji up its stone staircase, the clay-disc throw off the terrace, and Kozan-ji below.

Thu 19Kyoto · north

Ohara and Sanzen-in's moss garden, which also turns early, and Rurikoin up at Yase if you reserved it — the lacquer-table reflection is at its best before the city floor catches up. Kikunoi Honten in the evening.

Fri 20Kyoto · Arashiyama

Iwatayama monkey park at opening — macaques on a hilltop with the city behind them — then the bamboo grove and Tenryu-ji. Heki kintsugi in the afternoon; Eikandō's illumination is running by now, with the maples part-turned.

Sat 21Kyoto

Kurama and Kibune on the Eizan line — the lantern staircase at Kibune-jinja under red maples, and the momiji tunnel on the way up. Kodaiji Jugyuan farewell dinner and the illumination next door. Note: 21–23 November is a holiday weekend, so this last day is the busiest of the trip.

Sun 22✈

KIX→SFO Polaris, landing Sunday. Three clear days before Colorado.
