## Classic luxury (Golden Route)

### 1. Ultimate Japan — Scott Dunn

- **URL:** https://www.scottdunn.com/japan/tours/ultimate-japan
- **12 days, private tailor-made.** Tokyo (3n) → Hiroshima/Miyajima (2n) → Kyoto (3n) → Hakone (2n, **Gora Kadan**) → Tokyo (1n)
- **Hotels:** Prince Gallery Tokyo Kioicho, Gora Kadan, Ritz-Carlton Kyoto, Peninsula Tokyo. Upgrades: Aman Tokyo, Hoshinoya Kyoto, Hotel The Mitsui Kyoto
- **Price:** October 2026 guide £9,500 pp incl. flights (~$24k/couple) — **in budget**
- **Standouts:** Gora Kadan kaiseki stay, sushi masterclass after private Tsukiji tour, Mt Misen climb on Miyajima
- **Fit:** best budget-to-luxury ratio found; Gora Kadan is already on the seeds list

### 2. A Journey into Japan — Black Tomato

- **URL:** https://www.blacktomato.com/destinations/japan/a-journey-into-japan/
- **11 days.** Tokyo (4n) → Kanazawa/Kaga (1n) → Kyoto (4n) → Tokyo (1n, Aman)
- **Hotels:** Palace Hotel Tokyo, **Beniya Mukayu (Relais & Châteaux)**, Ritz-Carlton Kyoto, Aman Tokyo
- **Price:** from £16,500 pp excl. flights (~$42k/couple)
- **Standouts:** private sumo stable access, dinner attended by geisha, sunrise fish market + sushi lesson
- **Fit:** explicitly recommends **October–November**; R&C ryokan matches seeds; over budget as specced

### 3. Classic Japan — Remote Lands

- **URL:** https://www.remotelands.com/itinerary/classic-japan
- **14 days.** Tokyo (4n) → Kanazawa/Kaga (2n) → Kyoto (3n) → Naoshima (2n, Benesse House) → Osaka (2n)
- **Hotels:** Aman Tokyo, Beniya Mukayu/Kayotei, Aman Kyoto, Benesse House, Four Seasons Osaka
- **Price:** from $48,100 pp (~$96k/couple) — ultra-high anchor, not a candidate
- **Standouts:** market tour + sushi lesson with Michelin-starred chef, after-hours Benesse museum access
- **Fit:** exact 14-day shape and pacing worth copying at lower price point

### 4. Luxury Japan — Audley Travel

- **URL:** https://www.audleytravel.com/japan/tours/luxury-japan
- **16 days incl. flights (13 ground nights).** Tokyo (4n) → Kiso Valley/Nakasendo (2n) → Hiroshima (2n) → Kyoto (4n) → Hakone (2n, **Gora Kadan**) → Tokyo (1n)
- **Hotels:** Palace Hotel Tokyo, Four Seasons Kyoto (alt: Tawaraya), Gora Kadan, Mandarin Oriental Tokyo
- **Price:** from £14,435 pp incl. flights (~$36k/couple)
- **Standouts:** helicopter over Tokyo, sumo training + lunch with wrestlers, shop-and-cook dinner with Nishiki Market chef
- **Fit:** only classic itinerary with a rural walking segment (Nakasendo) — matches "remote mountain towns" seed

### 5. Luxury Japan: Art, Culture & Cuisine — Boutique Japan

- **URL:** https://boutiquejapan.com/luxury-japan-art-culture-cuisine-11-days/
- **11 days.** Tokyo (3n) → Hiroshima/Miyajima (2n) → Naoshima (1n, Benesse) → Kyoto (2n) → Hakone (1n, **Gora Kadan**) → depart
- **Price:** $1,800–2,200 pp/day (~$40–48k/couple)
- **Standouts:** sumo tournament tickets, sake-pairing dinner with expert, craftsperson workshop visit in Kyoto
- **Fit:** art + craft + Gora Kadan in one route; pace is brisk (5 bases in 11 days)

### 6. Classic Japan Explorer — Jacada Travel

- **URL:** https://www.jacadatravel.com/asia/trips/classic-japan-explorer-a-luxury-introduction-to-japan/
- **9 days.** Tokyo (3n, **Aman**) → Yamashiro Onsen (2n, Beniya Mukayu) → Kyoto (3n)
- **Price:** POA
- **Standouts:** private tea ceremony/calligraphy, kaiseki + private onsen baths
- **Fit:** cleanest minimal three-base structure; too short alone, good as a trip "core"

## Nature, wildlife & autumn

### 7. Autumn Splendour — InsideJapan Tours

- **URL:** https://www.insidejapantours.com/self-guided-japan-holidays/i-asc/autumn-splendour/
- **15 days.** Tokyo (3n) → Matsumoto (1n) → **Kamikochi (2n)** → Takayama (2n) → Shirakawago/Kanazawa (2n) → Kyoto (3n) → Tokyo (1n)
- **Price:** from $4,653 pp at Classic level; hotels upgradeable to 5-star via tailormade
- **Standouts:** Kamikochi foliage hiking (**peaks early October**; the one major alpine area timed right for an October trip), Kyoto night illuminations
- **Fit:** the route most purpose-built for October; swap in luxury ryokan and it hits nearly every seed

### 8. Japan's Far East 9-Day Wildlife & Adventure — Adventure Hokkaido

- **URL:** https://www.adventure-hokkaido.com/tours/japans-far-east-9-day-wildlife-adventure-tour
- **9 days, small group, Kushiro loop.** Nemuro → Rausu (whale/orca cruise) → **Shiretoko** (brown-bear boat cruise, Five Lakes hike) → Lake Akan (Ainu guide) → Kushiro wetlands (**permit-only red-crowned crane sanctuary hike**)
- **Price:** ¥585,000 pp (~$7.8k/couple); **Oct 8–16, 2026 departure had 1 space left** — private version would be needed
- **Standouts:** salmon-hunting brown bears from a small boat (autumn peak), orcas in Nemuro Strait, Kirakotan crane sanctuary
- **Fit:** directly hits Shiretoko wildlife seed; lodging is regional-comfortable, not 5-star. Note: cranes dancing in snow, Steller's eagles, and **Ezo flying squirrels are Dec–Mar** — their winter tour covers those
- **Caveat:** October = bears feeding on salmon runs, a genuine draw; but the full crane/squirrel wish list points at a second winter trip

### 9. Snow Monkeys & Samurai — Unforgettable Travel Company

- **URL:** https://unforgettabletravel.com/japan/snow-monkeys-samurai-winter-in-the-japanese-alps/
- **11 days.** Tokyo (1n) → Yatsugatake farmhouse (2n, exclusive-use Alex Kerr house) → Shibu Onsen (2n, 400-year-old Kokuya Ryokan) + **Jigokudani snow monkey day** → Kanazawa (2n) → Shirakawago/Takayama (2n) → Inuyama (1n)
- **Price:** from £7,220 pp (~$19.4k/couple) — in budget
- **Standouts:** exclusive-use restored farmhouse, master key to Shibu Onsen's nine bathhouses, snow monkeys
- **Fit:** monkeys are present in October but rarely bathe outside winter — operator is tailor-made and can re-season the route for autumn

### 10. Active Japan: Hiking & Biking — Remote Lands

- **URL:** https://www.remotelands.com/itinerary/active-japan---hiking-and-biking-extraordinary-japan
- **11 days.** Kyoto (3n, Aman Kyoto; invitation-only Kokedera moss temple) → Miyajima (1n) → Shikoku (3n, Setouchi Retreat Aonagi; **Shimanami Kaido 40-mile island cycle**) → Kurashiki (1n) → Kanazawa/Yamanaka Onsen (2n, Kayotei; Hakusan National Park trek)
- **Price:** from $40,100 pp (~$80k/couple) — ideas source, not a candidate
- **Fit:** strongest nature-meets-5-star pedigree; October is prime foliage in Hakusan/Kakusenkei Gorge

### 11. Luxury Ryokans & the Japanese Countryside — Boutique Japan

- **URL:** https://boutiquejapan.com/luxury-ryokans-and-the-japanese-countryside/
- **13 days.** Kyoto → Kurashiki → Naoshima (Benesse House) → Ise-Shima (**Amanemu**) → Shuzenji Onsen (**Asaba**) → Tokyo (Hoshinoya)
- **Price:** $1,800–2,200 pp/day (~$47–57k/couple)
- **Fit:** the ryokan-immersion archetype — five legendary properties in one route; over budget but the property list is a shopping list

### 12. Wild Japan in Winter — Naturetrek

- **URL:** https://www.naturetrek.co.uk/tours/wild-japan-in-winter

- **Fit:** ❌ for October — filed as the blueprint for a **future winter photography trip** that captures the full seeds wildlife list (cranes, snow monkeys, Ezo flying squirrel territory)

## Patterns worth stealing

1. **The consensus skeleton** across every classic itinerary: Tokyo 3–4n → onsen ryokan 1–2n → Kyoto 3–4n, with one "spoke" (Kanazawa, Naoshima, Hiroshima, or Japan Alps). A 14-day trip fits the skeleton plus **two** spokes comfortably.
2. **October is validated everywhere** — Black Tomato names Oct–Nov its best window; Kamikochi foliage peaks early October; Hokkaido has salmon-run bears; inland sea weather is ideal. Temps in Kyoto/Tokyo in October: ~57–72°F, matching the 55–75°F seed.
3. **Budget reality:** ~$24k/couple (Scott Dunn) buys 5-star hotels + selective private guiding incl. flights. Nightly luxury-ryokan + full private guiding pushes $35–50k. Self-guided luxury (InsideJapan tailormade, Wabunka modules) can hit the seeds list within $30k.
4. **Gora Kadan appears in 3 of 6 classic itineraries** — the seeds hotel pick is well-supported.
5. **The wildlife tension:** October delivers bears, whales, and foliage; cranes-in-snow, snow-monkeys-in-onsen, and Ezo flying squirrels peak Dec–Mar. Two realistic answers: a Hokkaido autumn module in this trip, or a dedicated winter photo trip later (itineraries 8, 12).
6. **Craft experiences cluster in Kanazawa and Kyoto** — both already on the route in every classic itinerary; Wabunka modules bolt on cleanly.
