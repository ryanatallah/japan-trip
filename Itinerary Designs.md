# Itinerary Designs — Oct 25 to Dec 3, 2026

Five self-guided itineraries built from [seeds](seeds.md) + fresh research (July 2026). All assume 2 travelers from SFO, business class lie-flat, 5-star/Relais & Châteaux/Michelin-Key hotels, GF accommodation for Ashly arranged at booking.

## The five at a glance

| # | Itinerary | Dates (Sat/Sun anchored) | Regions | Est. total (couple) | Temp band | Best for |
|---|-----------|--------------------------|---------|--------------------:|-----------|----------|
| 1 | Peak Momiji Classic | Sat Nov 14 → Sun Nov 29 | Tokyo → Hakone → Kyoto | $33–39k | ✅ 45–65°F | Peak foliage + crafts + the two seed restaurants |
| 2 | Crafts, Crab & Snow Monkeys | Sat Nov 7 → Sun Nov 22 | Tokyo → Nagano → Kanazawa/Kaga → Kyoto | $29–33k | ✅ mostly (Nagano 40s) | Hands-on crafts, crab season, monkeys |
| 3 | Wildlife North + Momiji Finale | Sat Nov 14 → Sun Nov 29 | Tokyo → E. Hokkaido → Kyoto | $29–33k | ⚠️ Hokkaido 20–40°F (flexed) | Ezo flying squirrel + cranes + peak Kyoto |
| 4 | Southern Warmth | Sat Oct 31 → Sun Nov 15 | Fukuoka → Kurokawa/Aso → Yakushima → Kyoto | $27–30k | ✅✅ 55–70°F throughout | Warmest; road trip; most in-budget |
| 5 | The Grand Tour (stretch) | Sat Nov 14 → Thu Dec 3 | Tokyo → Hokkaido → Kyoto → Hakone | $38–44k | ⚠️ mixed | Everything; breaks budget + Sat/Sun return |

**Date logic.** Kyoto's 2026 foliage is forecast to peak **Nov 25–Dec 7** (densest Nov 29–Dec 3) — later than average. So itineraries wanting peak Kyoto should end as late as possible, with Kyoto last. Kenrokuen (Kanazawa) peaks mid-Nov; Japan Alps peaked mid-Oct (Kamikochi closes Nov 15 — excluded); Kyushu runs early–late Nov; Hokkaido foliage is over — there it's pre-winter wildlife season. Crane feeding at Tsurui starts mid-to-late Nov; Ezo momonga are viewable year-round with a guide. Kyoto crowds are worst Nov 15–25 (incl. Nov 21–23 holiday weekend); the Nov 29–Dec 3 tail has peak color with the crowd wave passing.

## Shared foundations

### Flights from SFO (~$9,000–11,000/couple business, RT)

- **Best product: ANA "The Room"** on SFO→HND — 28"-wide lie-flat with closing door. SFO frequencies alternate between new and old configs; **verify the seat map before ticketing**. ~$4,500–5,500 pp booked 3–6 months out.
- **Open-jaw into Tokyo, out of Osaka** (itineraries 1, 3, 4 end near Kyoto): United is the only SFO–KIX nonstop; a single United multi-city ticket (SFO→HND / KIX→SFO, Polaris) prices within ~$0–500 of a normal RT. Saves a half-day backtrack to Tokyo.
- JAL from SFO still flies its older Sky Suite (the new A350 suite isn't on SFO yet). Consolidator fares run $3,400–4,800 RT if you want to shave $2k.
- Depart SFO Saturday morning → land Japan Sunday afternoon (date line). Return flights land at SFO the same day they leave Japan — a Sunday departure from Japan is a Sunday arrival home.

### Rail vs self-drive — the honest trade-offs

| Factor | Green Car rail | Self-drive |
|---|---|---|
| Comfort/luxury | Shinkansen Green/Gran Class ≈ domestic first class; 2h15 Tokyo–Kyoto | Lexus NX/RX rents officially from Toyota Rent a Car ($230–410/day); roads excellent |
| Freedom | Fixed but very frequent departures | Wins in rural areas; stop anywhere |
| Luggage | The real catch — big bags need reserved oversize space. Solution: **takkyubin** forwarding, $15–22/bag next-day hotel-to-hotel; travel with overnight bags | Trunk swallows everything |
| Cities | Wins outright | Tokyo/Kyoto parking $50–90/day, foliage gridlock, narrow one-ways — a burden |
| Safety/stress | Zero | Left-side driving; IDP required (**1949 Geneva booklet from AAA, ~$20** — card versions rejected); Hokkaido late Nov = black ice, 4pm sunsets, studless tires (fitted free on all Hokkaido rentals) |
| Verdict | **Cities + trunk routes** | **Rural loops: E. Hokkaido (essentially car-only), Kyushu onsen country, Yakushima** |

The hybrid pattern — rail/fly between hubs, rent for 2–4-day rural loops, takkyubin the bags past the loop — appears in itineraries 2, 3, 4. Private Alphard day-hire in Kyoto/Tokyo ($300–500/day) is the no-stress option for temple-hopping days.

### Gluten-free playbook (Ashly)

- **Kikunoi Honten has a formal GF intake** on its reservation form ("guests requiring gluten-free meals..."). Declare at booking — no same-day dietary requests. **L'Effervescence (Tokyo, 3★)** prints a personalized GF menu. **Sézanne explicitly refuses GF — avoid.** Monk is pizza-centric — skip.
- **Gora Kadan** has celiac guest reviews confirming full GF kaiseki + breakfast (staying guests only). Beniya Mukayu, Asaba, and Sankara Yakushima all accommodate with advance notice; Sankara publishes GF options outright.
- Strategy: tamari instead of shoyu (request at booking; carry travel packets), yakitori shio not tare, teppanyaki salt-grilled, sushi minus nikiri/marinated pieces; never tempura, ramen, udon, unagi. Carry a Japanese-language celiac card (Legal Nomads). Backup: Gluten Free T's Kitchen Tokyo (certified GF — GF tempura exists there), Little Bird Kyoto.
- Send every ryokan/restaurant the GF brief 4–6 weeks ahead; a detailed reply is the signal the kitchen can be trusted.

### Cost model assumptions

Per-couple estimates: business flights $9–11k; ryokan rates include kaiseki dinner + breakfast (HB); city hotels room/breakfast only; dining line covers all meals not included in hotels (marquee dinners $250–900/couple each); experiences priced from operator pages; 5–8% buffer not included. FX ¥150/$.

## Itinerary 1 — Peak Momiji Classic

**Sat Nov 14 → Sun Nov 29 · Tokyo (4n) → Hakone (2n) → Kyoto (7n) · open-jaw KIX return**
**Transport: rail.** This route never leaves the shinkansen spine — a car would sit in $70/day parking through peak-season gridlock. Green Car via smartEX app; takkyubin bags Tokyo→Kyoto past the Hakone stop; private transfers on arrival/departure days.

| Mon 16 | Tokyo | Meiji Jingu + Omotesando; icho (ginkgo) starting to turn; evening yakitori shio counter |
| Tue 17 | Tokyo (Ome) | **Hirata sword smithing** — forge a small katana with the husband-wife smiths, engraved + shipped home (150 min, ~$1,700/couple). The exact experience from seeds, Ashly-approved 👍 |
| Wed 18 | Tokyo | **Mutoh modern kintsugi, Nihonbashi** (90 min, ~$460/couple) — take the repaired piece home same-day; teamLab or Yanaka stroll; **L'Effervescence** dinner (3★, printed GF menu, ~$1,200/couple) |

| Sat 21 | Kyoto | Green Car Odawara→Kyoto; check in **Sowaka** (SLH, Gion, ~$1,200–1,600/n peak) or **Hotel The Mitsui** (3 Keys, +$1–3k/n). Evening Gion walk |
| Sun 22 | Kyoto | Early (8am) Honen-in/Philosopher's Path before crowds; **Kodaiji Jugyuan** dinner (2★, seed restaurant, ~$700/couple) — literally next to Kodaiji's night illumination |

**Levers to ~$33k:** United/consolidator fares (−$2k), swap 2 craft modules out (−$2k), Hakone Gora Byakudan instead of Gora Kadan (−$1.3k). Kyoto at the Mitsui instead of Sowaka adds $7–20k. This is the trip the seeds describe most literally. It now lands about $8k over the top of budget: peak-Kyoto November is Japan's most expensive room-night of the year, and the Aman swap adds $2k on top of that. **Book Kyoto hotels and Kikunoi immediately** (peak rooms sell ~12 weeks out, and desirable dates 9–12 months out).

## Itinerary 2 — Crafts, Crab & Snow Monkeys

**Sat Nov 7 → Sun Nov 22 · Tokyo (3n) → Shibu Onsen (2n) → Kanazawa/Kaga (3n) → Kyoto (5n) → KIX out**
**Transport: rail + one car day.** Hokuriku shinkansen **Gran Class** (attendant service, ~$190 Tokyo→Kanazawa leg) is the country's best train seat and only exists on this line — the route is built around it. Local Nagaden line + taxi covers the monkey park; no rental needed.

## Itinerary 3 — Wildlife North + Momiji Finale

**Sat Nov 14 → Sun Nov 29 · Tokyo (2n) → E. Hokkaido self-drive (4n) → Kyoto (6n) → KIX out**
**Transport: fly + self-drive Hokkaido loop + rail.** Eastern Hokkaido is effectively car-only and is where your road-trip instincts pay off: empty, beautiful roads. Rentals come with studless tires fitted free; drive daylight only (sunset ~4pm), watch dawn black ice. HND→Kushiro 1h40 (~$100–125 pp); Kushiro→HND→ITM to reach Kyoto.

| Day | Where | Plan |
|---|---|---|
| Sat 14 | ✈ | SFO→HND |
| Sun 15 | Tokyo | Land; Aman Tokyo 1n; recovery evening |
| Mon 16 | Tokyo | **Mutoh kintsugi** morning; afternoon HND→Kushiro; pick up 4WD; **Akan Tsuruga Bessou Hinanoza**, Lake Akan (25 suites, all private onsen — the honest luxury ceiling out east, ~$850/n HB) |
| Tue 17 | Akan/Tsurui | **Red-crowned cranes at Tsurui-Ito Sanctuary** — feeding season starts mid-to-late Nov; numbers building, not yet the Jan–Feb 300-bird spectacle. Ural owl roost + Ezo deer/fox with local guide |
| Wed 18 | Kussharo | **Whooper swans arriving at Lake Kussharo** (Kotan/Sunayu lakeside onsen); Mt. Io; second Hinanoza night |
| Thu 19 | Kushiro | Kushiro-Shitsugen wetland boardwalks; dusk: **Ezo flying squirrel session with Kushiro Trek & Guide** (Ashly's #1 seed, 👍×5) — momonga emerge ~10 min after sunset at monitored burrows; guides run these year-round with high success at known dens (~$200–300/couple). Overnight Kushiro or 3rd Hinanoza night |
| Fri 20 | → Kyoto | Morning momonga backup slot (weather insurance — windy/rainy nights get cancelled); fly Kushiro→HND→ITM; **Sowaka** Kyoto (~$1,300/n peak) |
| Sat 21–Fri 27 | Kyoto | The Itinerary-1 Kyoto week compressed: **Kikunoi**, **Jugyuan**, **Heki kintsugi**, **Shoraku Raku**, **kyudo**, **Uji matcha**, Eikando/Rurikoin, with the final days riding into forecast peak color |
| Sat 28 | Kyoto | Peak-color free day; **Cenci** farewell |
| Sun 29 | ✈ | KIX→SFO |

| Cost (couple) | Est. |
|---|---:|
| Flights international | $9,500 |
| Domestic flights (HND–Kushiro, Kushiro–ITM via HND) | $900 |
| Hotels: Aman 1n + Hinanoza 3n (HB) + Kushiro 1n + Sowaka 6n | $12,500 |
| Car (4WD 4 days + fuel/tolls) | $700 |
| Dining | $3,600 |
| Experiences + guides | $3,300 |
| Ground | $900 |
| **Total** | **≈ $31,400** |

**Honesty box:** Shiretoko is deliberately absent — the pass closes early Nov, Five Lakes trails close late Nov, bear cruises are done, and drift-ice eagles are a February show. Late Nov delivers momonga, arriving cranes, swans, owls, foxes — genuinely good, but the iconic winter postcards (crane courtship in snow, Otowa Bridge mist) need a Jan–Feb trip. If squirrels alone justify Hokkaido for you, this itinerary works; if you want the full winter spectacle, do Itinerary 1 or 2 now and bank a dedicated February photo trip.

## Itinerary 4 — Southern Warmth

**Sat Oct 31 → Sun Nov 15 · Fukuoka (2n) → Kurokawa Onsen (2n) → Kagoshima (1n) → Yakushima (3n) → Kyoto (6n) · open-jaw KIX return**
**Transport: fly + self-drive Kyushu.** The truest road-trip successor to your France/UK/Germany format: a Lexus through Aso caldera country is one of Japan's great drives, and rural onsen are awkward without a car. Warmest itinerary — 55–70°F nearly throughout; the only one fully inside the seeds temp band.

|---|---|---|

| Wed 4 | Aso | Aso caldera + Kusasenri grasslands drive — the road-trip day; Kurokawa town stroll in yukata, rotenburo-hopping pass |

## Itinerary 5 — The Grand Tour (stretch)

**Sat Nov 14 → Thu Dec 3 · Tokyo (3n) → E. Hokkaido (3n) → Kyoto (6n) → Hakone (2n) → Tokyo out · 19 days**
**Transport: fly + Hokkaido self-drive + rail.** The everything trip: momonga and cranes *and* densest peak-Kyoto color (Nov 29–Dec 3 forecast max) *and* Gora Kadan. It exists to show what the window physically allows.

Day flow: Tokyo (Hirata sword, Mutoh kintsugi, L'Effervescence) → fly Kushiro, Hinanoza + Tsurui cranes + momonga session (as It.3) → fly to Kyoto for six nights riding into the forecast crest (Kikunoi, Jugyuan, Raku, indigo, kyudo, Uji matcha, illuminations) → Gora Kadan for two kaiseki nights → HND→SFO Thu Dec 3.

| Cost (couple) | Est. |
|---|---:|
| Flights intl + domestic | $10,800 |
| Hotels (17 nights: Aman 3 + Hinanoza 3 + Sowaka 6 + Gora Kadan 2 + Tokyo 1, HB where noted) | $21,000 |
| Dining / experiences / car / ground | $9,200 |
| **Total** | **≈ $41,000** |

**Breaks two seed rules** — budget ($38–44k) and the Sat/Sun return (lands Thu Dec 3). Included because it's the only single-trip answer to "peak Kyoto + flying squirrels + Gora Kadan." Trim to budget by cutting Hakone (−$3.5k) and two craft modules (−$2k) → ~$35.5k.

## Recommendation logic

- **Want the seeds trip as written** (Kikunoi, Gora Kadan, kintsugi, peak beauty): **Itinerary 1**, booked this month.
- **Want maximum hands-on craft + best food-season alignment**: **Itinerary 2** (crab season + Kenrokuen peak are real, underrated wins).
- **Ashly's flying squirrel is non-negotiable this trip**: **Itinerary 3** — with the honest caveat that Hokkaido's postcard winter is Jan–Feb, arguing for **Itinerary 1 or 2 now + a February wildlife trip later** (Naturetrek-style, ~$19k, from the inspiration doc).
- **Budget discipline + warmth + road trip**: **Itinerary 4** — the most under-$30k headroom and the only all-temp-band option.
- **Once-in-a-decade mood**: **Itinerary 5**.

## Book-now list (for a Nov 2026 trip, acting in July–Sep 2026)

1. **Now:** Kyoto hotels for late Nov (peak rooms go 9–12 wks+ out; Tawaraya/Aman-class 9–12 months); Gora Kadan; Hinanoza; Sankara; Beniya Mukayu. Flights (ANA Room seat-map check).
2. **Aug–early Sep:** Kikunoi Honten (Tableall/My Concierge, declare GF in the form), L'Effervescence (Pocket Concierge), Zeniya. Wabunka modules (Hirata calendar is irregular — ask early), Shoraku kiln (one group/day).
3. **Fixed windows:** Goh = email exactly 60 days out; Den (if added) = 1 month out; kyudo studio = email to confirm location/dates.
4. **4–6 weeks out:** GF briefs to every kitchen (celiac-specific, tamari request, fryer question); takkyubin plan; AAA 1949-format IDP if driving.
